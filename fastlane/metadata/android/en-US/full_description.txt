<b>Audile</b> is an open-source music recognition app that can help you quickly and accurately identify a music track playing near you.
The app integrates AudD, ACRCloud, and Shazam for song identification, and uses Odesli to retrieve additional platform-specific track links.

<b>Features:</b>

<b>Recognition</b> - Audile allows you to recognize a track in one tap. The app can record from the microphone or from device audio, including music playing through headphones. If you are offline, the recording can be saved and recognition continues once you are back online.
<b>Auto recognition</b> - The app can run recognition in the background at intervals and notify you of each match as it is found. This feature uses only the microphone.
<b>Background usage</b> - Use the app seamlessly from the notification drawer, a home screen widget, a floating button, or a quick settings tile. Start recognition and get the result without launching the app.
<b>Track information</b> - After a successful recognition, the app searches for lyrics and for links to the track on music services. Recognized tracks are kept in the library, where you can search them or mark them as favorites.
<b>Backup and preferences</b> - App data can be backed up and restored, and tracks can be exported to a CSV file. The app also offers various options to customize its behavior and appearance.
